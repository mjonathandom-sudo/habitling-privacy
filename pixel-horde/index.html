Pixel Horde Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

App: Pixel Horde for Android, closed test, version 1.16.0 (version code 40)

Package: com.mjdom.pixelhorde

Developer: MJ Dom

Pixel Horde stores your game on your device. This closed-test build has no Pixel Horde account, and it does not run live ads or live purchases. Information reaches the developer only if you open Send feedback and then press Send in your own email app.

What this page covers

This policy describes the closed-test build named above. It is a separate page from the Habitling privacy policy.

The game still contains on-device placeholder buttons for a rewarded ad and a one-time Premium unlock, and the About text says a privacy policy will be added with a later store release. Those are not live data collection in version 1.16.0 (version code 40). If a later build adds an advertising SDK, store billing, an account, analytics, or any other collection or sharing, this policy will be updated, with a new effective date, so that the page matches that build.

Who we are

Pixel Horde is developed under the name MJ Dom. The Android package name is com.mjdom.pixelhorde. Privacy questions and feedback go to conradimatt@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The game keeps one save in the app’s private storage, in WebView local storage under the key pixelhorde.save.v1 (save version 19). MJ Dom has no server that receives this save, and we cannot open the copy on your phone.

The save can include:

  • Progress, such as level, experience, skill points, gold, village, stash, armory, resources, kits, raids, heroes, bestiary, and tutorial flags.
  • Settings, such as sound, music, banners, clean or verbose HUD, and loot filters.
  • A local premium flag and a local cheer count, if you have used the on-device placeholders described below.

This build does not add its own encryption to that save. A phone may protect private app storage with the device’s own encryption and screen lock. That depends on the device. This policy does not promise that every phone does so.

The app allows Android backup. If device backup is switched on, Android may include Pixel Horde’s app data in your personal backup. If you use Google’s backup, Google handles that copy under your Google account. We do not operate that backup, and we do not promise that no backup copy exists.

Optional feedback

The hub and the pause screen each have a Feedback control. Using it is optional.

You can type a note of up to 800 characters. The game also tries to capture a PNG of its own canvas (the game image, not a picture of other apps or of the whole device). On this Android build, Send opens your email app with a message addressed to conradimatt@gmail.com. Pixel Horde does not upload that message. We receive it only if you press Send in the email app. If you cancel, or if the phone has no email app, we do not receive the feedback.

If you press Send, the email can include:

  • The note you typed.
  • A subject line with the app name and version, and a short footer with Pixel Horde 1.16.0 (version code 40), the screen you were on, the wave if a run is in progress, the selected map, and the selected hero.
  • The canvas PNG, when capture succeeded. The Android feedback plugin writes that image to the app cache and grants the email app you choose temporary permission to read that one file.
  • The From address of the mailbox you send from. Your email app adds that address. We do not read your contacts to choose it.

Your email app and your email provider then process the message under their own policies. The copy we can read is the mailbox for conradimatt@gmail.com. We use it to understand closed-test comments, fix problems, and reply if you asked us to. We do not sell it, and we do not use it to advertise to you.

Do not put sensitive information in feedback. That includes passwords, payment card numbers, government identifiers, health details, and private information about other people. The picture shows the game canvas, so avoid leaving private text on that screen if you do not want it in the email. Feedback is an ordinary email. It is not a secure form controlled by Pixel Horde. Whether the message is protected in transit depends on your email app and provider.

A copy of the PNG may remain in the app cache until you clear the app cache or storage, or uninstall the app. This build does not set a fixed time limit on that file.

Permissions and software in this build

The Android manifest declares the INTERNET permission. This closed-test build runs the game from files packaged inside the app. The game code in this build does not send your save, analytics, advertisements, or purchases to a Pixel Horde server.

The manifest does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, phone, notification, or storage permissions. The feedback image stays in the app cache and is shared only with the app you pick when you send.

The shipped build includes Capacitor, which hosts the local game, AndroidX interface libraries, and a first-party feedback plugin that opens the email compose screen. The dependency list for this build does not include an advertising SDK, Google Play Billing, Firebase, a crash reporter, or an analytics SDK. The shipped source archive does not include a Google services configuration file. This policy describes those services as absent from this build.

The audited code does not read an advertising ID, a persistent device identifier such as an IMEI, or your contact list.

Ads and purchases

Live advertising and live billing are off in this build. The in-game switch that would call an ad or billing plugin is false, and those plugins are not installed. Settings and the support screen can still open local placeholder dialogs about watching an ad or unlocking Premium. Confirming a placeholder can change the local save on your device, including the local premium flag or the cheer count. It does not contact an ad network, does not contact Google Play Billing, and does not charge money.

Version 1.16.0 (version code 40) has no live ads and no live in-app purchases. A later build that turns those on needs an updated policy.

Google Play

This build is distributed for Google Play closed testing. Google processes your Play account, device, and install under Google’s privacy policy. The game does not read your Google account. Google may show closed-test participation to the developer in Play Console, because that is how Google runs a closed test.

Why information is used

  • On the device: to run the game, keep progress, and apply your settings.
  • Feedback email, only after you press Send: to read closed-test comments, fix problems, and reply.
  • Google Play: to deliver the closed test. That processing is Google’s.

We do not sell personal information.

How long information is kept

We do not promise a fixed number of days.

  • The on-device save remains until you reset it, clear app storage, or uninstall, unless a device backup later restores a copy.
  • The feedback image in the app cache remains until the cache or the app is removed. There is no fixed timer.
  • A feedback email remains in the mailbox we can access until that copy is deleted. Your email provider, and Google when the message went through Gmail or Play, may keep their own copies under their policies. We cannot delete those copies for them.

Deleting information

Reset, uninstall, and deleting a feedback email are different actions. This build does not create a Pixel Horde account, so there is no account to delete.

Reset Progress is in Settings. It asks you to confirm, then to type WIPE. It removes the saved game from local storage and writes a new save. If the local premium flag was already on, Reset keeps that flag. Reset does not delete an email you already sent, and it does not remove the feedback image from the app cache. Skills Respec only refunds skill points. It leaves the rest of the save in place.

Uninstall, or Android Settings, Apps, Pixel Horde, Storage, Clear storage, removes the app’s local data from the phone, including the save and the cache image. Clear cache removes cache files and leaves the save. If Android backup is on, a later install can restore a backup copy. We cannot delete that backup from this page.

Feedback email. To ask us to delete a message you sent, email conradimatt@gmail.com and identify the message. We will delete the copy we can still find in that mailbox. That request leaves the on-device save as it is. Reset and uninstall leave the mailbox copy as it is.

Children

This closed test is not directed at children. The game does not ask for a date of birth. If you believe a child sent feedback, email conradimatt@gmail.com and ask us to delete it.

Habitling Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026

Habitling is a habit tracker where each habit gets a small pet. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Habitling Android app.

The short version

Habitling does not collect, send or sell any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your habits, pet names, check-ins, streaks, reminder times and settings are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage.
  • There is no account, no sign-in and no Habitling server. We cannot see your data.
  • Habitling contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications, scheduled on your phone by Android. They are not sent through the internet or any push service. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in Android Settings.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show your habit reminders.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms): optional, so reminders arrive on time. If you don't grant it, reminders may arrive a few minutes late.
  • Run at startup: to put your scheduled reminders back after the phone restarts or the app updates.

Backups and exports

  • If you tap Export, Habitling creates a backup file and hands it to the app you choose (for example Files, Drive or a chat app). What happens to that file afterwards is up to you and that app's own privacy policy.
  • Import only reads a file you pick.
  • Android's own device backup (if you have it switched on) may include Habitling's data in your personal Google backup. That is managed by Android and your Google account, not by us.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Habitling, or clearing its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Habitling → Storage, deletes all of its data from your phone. There is nothing stored anywhere else for us to delete.

Children

Habitling is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
